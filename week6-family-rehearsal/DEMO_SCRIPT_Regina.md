# DEMO SCRIPT — Rehearsal MX

## 0:00–0:20 — Opening
Show the landing screen. Say: “Rehearsal MX does not teach earthquake information. It rehearses what happens when a family plan stops applying.” Point to the `SIMULACIÓN 3D · ACADÉMICA` label.

## 0:20–0:45 — Safety boundary
Point to the warning: this is not emergency advice and does not replace school or Protección Civil protocols.

## 0:45–1:30 — Scenario 1
Start the rehearsal. Read the changed constraint. Choose the option that confirms the school's status before acting. Mention that the goal is recognition and reassessment, not speed.

## 1:30–2:15 — Adaptive route
Show that the next scenario is selected from the remaining scenarios based on the previous decision. Point to `IA / lógica adaptativa: SIMULADA`. Use `Escuchar` to demonstrate browser voice.

## 2:15–2:50 — Scenario 2/3
Complete the remaining scenarios. Explain that the scenarios progressively test whether the user notices when assumptions become invalid rather than memorizing a fixed plan.

## 2:50–3:20 — Reflection
Show the result. Point out that the score is explicitly simulated and is not a prediction of safety. Show the transfer action: one concrete family responsibility and one alternate coordination channel.

## 3:20–3:30 — What changed my mind
Say: “I started with VR as the interesting part. The debate changed the hypothesis: the technology only earns its place if it creates measurable incremental adaptive behavior over a low-cost rehearsal. This MVP tests the mechanism first.”
